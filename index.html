Frequently asked questions

A few useful details before you start.

Is 4K Quality free?

Yes. This version is a free browser-based tool and does not require an account.

Are my images uploaded anywhere?

No. Image decoding, resizing, and exporting are performed in your browser. The site does not send your selected image to a processing server.

Does resizing make a low-resolution image truly 4K?

It can enlarge an image to 4K pixel dimensions, but ordinary resizing does not restore real detail. This tool does not claim to perform AI super-resolution.

Which formats can I export?

You can export as WebP, JPG, or PNG. The source image must be a format your browser can decode.

Will compression always make a file smaller?

Not always. The result depends on the source image, selected format, and quality setting. PNG can be larger than the original in some cases.
